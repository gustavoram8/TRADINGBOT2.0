01The part nobody teaches

You can know everything — and still freeze.

Trading is two skills wearing one name. The first — structure, levels, timing — you can study. The second can't be taught from a slide.

Patience while a trade breathes. Taking the loss without flinching. Not chasing, not revenging, not abandoning the plan the moment it gets uncomfortable. That second skill is the mind — and almost nobody teaches it, so most traders leave it to chance.

Then they wonder why they know exactly what to do… and do the opposite when it's live. The hardest part is that you usually can't see your own blind spot. The one thing self-study can never give you is someone who already walked through it — and can see it for you.
