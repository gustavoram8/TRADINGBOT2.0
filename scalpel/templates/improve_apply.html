04How it works

The full picture — before you decide.

No surprises and no fine print. This is how each program works, how each one is paid, and what we ask of you before the door opens.

Program 01 · On your own time

Pre-recorded classes & live trading

A private, growing library of recorded sessions where real analysis is taken apart from start to finish — the read, the reasoning, the mistakes, the why. New sessions are added regularly, and everything stays available for as long as you're a member.

How it's paid

A simple monthly subscription. While it's active you have the complete program — everything already recorded and everything new that gets added. Cancel whenever you want: your access simply runs to the end of the period you already paid. No contracts, no lock-in.

Program 02 · Up close

One-to-one online meetings

Private 30-minute sessions, one on one. You bring your charts, your decisions and your doubts; the time is spent on your trading, not on generic theory. It's the fastest way to find the thing you can't see on your own.

How it's paid

Monthly packages of 3 or 6 meetings — or a single meeting whenever you need one. Calendar time is finite, so seats are limited: a package is what reserves yours.

Who's behind it

A working trader — not a content creator.

The person on the other side is in the markets every single day, and teaches only what he actually does with his own capital. No borrowed strategies, no recycled courses. His name, his story and his full presentation are shared once your application is reviewed — attention is the scarcest resource in this room, and it's reserved for people who are serious.

The application

Tell us where you are.

Seats are few and attention is expensive. A short application keeps the room serious — answer honestly, and you'll get an honest answer back.
