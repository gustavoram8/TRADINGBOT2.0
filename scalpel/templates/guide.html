User Guide

A practical walkthrough of every tool in Tradeable — what each one is for and how to use it, step by step. This guide is educational only; nothing here is financial advice.

Analyze Your Setup

Upload a screenshot of your chart and our AI highlights possible setup errors and educational observations to reflect on — never a verdict, signal, or recommendation.

  1. Open the Analyze tab and pick your instrument, direction, session and any confluences using the buttons.
  2. Upload a screenshot of your chart. Use the summary panel on the right to confirm everything is ready.
  3. Press Analyze and read the observations. Treat them as prompts to review your own reasoning, not as instructions.

Pre-Flight

A pre-trade checklist so you confirm a setup actually meets your own rules before you commit to it.

  1. Open the Pre-Flight tab before you take a trade.
  2. Work through each checklist item honestly — bias, session, risk and invalidation.
  3. Only proceed when the checklist is clean. If something is missing, that is useful information too.

Quiz & Daily Challenge

Test your knowledge of the methodologies with a large question bank, and take one ultra-hard question every day to keep a streak.

  1. Open the Quiz tab and choose a topic or difficulty to start a session.
  2. Answer each question — the server checks your answer, so results are honest and never revealed early.
  3. Come back for the Daily Challenge: one hard question a day that can earn you rewards.

Forum

A Premium community space to share setups, ask questions and discuss trading. Every post is automatically moderated.

  1. Open Forum Trading and browse the feed, or switch to Saved to revisit posts.
  2. Use New post to share a setup or question. Keep it respectful and on topic.
  3. React and reply to other traders to build the discussion.

Chalkboard

A free-form drawing board to sketch setups, mark levels and annotate ideas, then save your boards for later.

  1. Open the Chalkboard tab and pick a drawing tool.
  2. Sketch your levels, zones and notes directly on the board.
  3. Your board is saved automatically in this browser so you can return to it.

Synapse

An interactive 3D knowledge hub. A holographic mind branches into every major methodology — explore them visually.

  1. Open the Synapse tab and wait for the neural matrix to load.
  2. Click a node to open its methodology and read the classified dossier.
  3. Use the Menu arrow to return to the rest of the app at any time.

Market Timing

Live session clocks (kill zones) and an economic calendar so you always know which session is active and what news is ahead.

  1. On the Analyze tab, find the Market Timing panel with the session bands.
  2. Hover a band to see its exact time in ET and your local time.
  3. Filter the economic calendar by your instrument to focus on relevant news.

Plans & Limits

Each plan sets how many screenshots you can analyze and unlocks different tools. You can see your remaining analyses in the right-hand panel.

  1. Free — 1 analysis every 7 days, to try the core tool.
  2. Standard — 1 analysis every 24 hours.
  3. Premium — 5 analyses every 24 hours, plus Forum, Quiz, Synapse and more.

Open Products → Plans in the sidebar to compare and upgrade at any time.

Account & Settings

Manage your profile, plan, language and theme, and control your session.

  1. Use the account panel (top of the right rail) to reach your plan, coupons and rank.
  2. Change language and light/dark theme from the sidebar controls.
  3. Open Products → Settings for account options.
