User Guide

Analyze Your Setup

Upload a screenshot of your chart and the AI walks through it the way an instructor would: what structure it can see, whether the timing and the confluences you claimed line up, and where the reasoning has holes. It never tells you to take the trade — it gives you things to check.

  1. Open the Analyze tab and set the context first: instrument, direction, session, and the confluences you believe are present. The AI reads a chart very differently depending on what you claim to be seeing, so this is not a formality.
  2. Pick the methodology you are trading. Each one has its own reading of the same candles — an order block matters under ICT and means nothing under Wyckoff — so choosing the wrong one produces an answer about a method you are not using.
  3. Upload a screenshot. Include enough chart to show where price came from: a zoomed-in image of the entry alone hides the context the analysis depends on. Marking your levels before the screenshot helps.
  4. Optionally write your Trade Construction — your reasoning in your own words, up to about 200 words. This is the part most people skip and it is the part that turns the answer from generic into specific to your idea.
  5. Press Analyze, then read it as a second opinion, not a verdict. The useful question is not "does it agree with me" but "did it see something I did not".
  6. Save the analysis to a project to keep a record. Reading three of your own old analyses side by side shows patterns in your mistakes that no single analysis can.

The counter on the right shows how many analyses you have left in the current window. It updates the moment an analysis finishes, so you never have to guess — and a failed upload does not consume one.

Pre-Flight

A checklist you fill in before you enter, not after. Its whole purpose is to make you state your rules while you still have nothing at risk, because the moment money is on the line your reasoning changes without asking permission.

  1. Open Pre-Flight and create a checklist for the setup you are looking at. Do it before you click buy or sell — a checklist filled in after entry is a diary, not a filter.
  2. Work through each item honestly: bias, session, risk and invalidation. Invalidation is the one that matters most: if you cannot say what would prove you wrong, you do not have a trade, you have an opinion.
  3. Answer with what is actually on the chart, not with what you hope. The checklist only works if it can tell you no.
  4. If an item does not pass, that is a result, not a failure. A setup that fails your own rules is information you paid nothing for.
  5. Keep your checklists. Going back over the ones you overrode is the fastest way to find out which rule you keep breaking.

Pre-Flight is a Premium tool, and you can keep up to ten checklists — enough to cover the setups you actually trade rather than every idea you look at.

Quiz & Daily Challenge

A question bank across the methodologies the platform teaches, in rising levels of difficulty, plus one very hard question a day. It is designed to find the gaps you did not know you had — not to make you feel good about what you already know.

  1. Open the Quiz tab and pick a methodology and a topic — order blocks, market structure, accumulation, chart patterns and so on.
  2. Choose a level. Beginner checks definitions, intermediate checks whether you can apply them, and advanced puts two rules in conflict so you have to know which one wins.
  3. Answer, then read the explanation even when you got it right — a lucky guess and real knowledge look identical on the score.
  4. Try a hardcore scenario when a topic feels easy: full situations with several plausible readings and only one that survives scrutiny.
  5. Come back for the Daily Challenge: one hard question every day, and a streak that only grows if you keep showing up.

Your answers are checked on the server and the options are shuffled, so the answer key is never in the page and there is no pattern to exploit. Your daily question is also rotated per account — nobody can pass you the answer, because theirs is a different one.

Forum

A space to post setups, ask what you got wrong and follow traders whose reasoning you want to learn from. Available on Standard and Premium. Every post and image passes automatic moderation before it is published.

  1. Open Forum and read the general feed, or the Following tab to see only the traders you follow.
  2. Use New post to share a setup or a question. Posting the chart with your reasoning gets you far better answers than posting the chart alone.
  3. Join or create a community for a methodology or a market, and post there when the topic is narrow enough that the general feed would bury it.
  4. React and reply to other traders. Answering someone else forces you to put your own reasoning into words, which is where the gaps show up.
  5. Use Saved to keep posts worth rereading, and Messages for a private conversation with another trader.

Moderation runs before publishing, not after complaints, and it covers text and images. It is there so nobody has to scroll past insults or signal-selling to reach the useful discussion.

Chalkboard

A free drawing board for thinking with your hands: sketch the structure, mark the levels, write the plan next to it. Useful precisely because it is not a chart — nothing moves while you think.

  1. Open the Chalkboard tab and pick a tool from the toolbar.
  2. Sketch the structure, zones and levels you are watching, and write the reasoning next to the drawing instead of keeping it in your head.
  3. Use several boards to separate ideas — one per instrument, or one per session — rather than piling everything onto one.
  4. Export the board when it is worth keeping. That exported file is the real save.

Your boards live in this browser, not in your account. They survive closing the tab, but they do not travel to your phone and they disappear if you clear site data. If a board matters, export it — that is the only copy that is genuinely yours.

Synapse

An interactive map of every methodology the platform covers, built as a 3D brain: each branch is a method, each node a concept, and each node opens a full dossier. It is for exploring how ideas connect, which a list of lessons cannot show you.

  1. Open Synapse and let the map load, then click a branch to open a methodology.
  2. Click a node to read its dossier: what the concept is, how it is identified, and how it relates to the concepts next to it.
  3. Follow the connections rather than reading in order. Seeing that two methods describe the same behaviour with different names is the point of the map.
  4. Everything is available in your language — the map, the names and the dossiers all follow the language you picked.
  5. Use the Menu arrow to return to the rest of the app whenever you want.

If you prefer to read away from the screen, the complete library is available as a PDF from the store. It is the same content, laid out for reading start to finish.

Market Timing

Live clocks for the sessions that actually move the instruments you trade, plus the economic calendar. Most avoidable losses are not bad analysis — they are good analysis at the wrong hour.

  1. On the Analyze tab, open the Market Timing panel to see which session is running right now.
  2. Hover any band to read its exact time in New York time and your own local time. The bands are the same for everyone; only the clock is yours.
  3. Learn the character of each window: the London and New York opens tend to expand, the lunch hours tend to chop, and the ranges built overnight are what the day session reaches for.
  4. Filter the economic calendar by your instrument so you only see the releases that can move it.
  5. Check what is scheduled before you plan an entry, not after price has already jumped.

A session clock tells you when participation usually shows up, not that something will happen. Holiday weeks keep the same clock with almost none of the volume behind it.

Plans & Limits

Your plan sets how often you can run an analysis, how many projects you can keep, and which tools are unlocked. Everything else — the guide, the map, your rank — is the same for everyone.

  1. Free — one analysis every 7 days and one saved project. Enough to see exactly what the tool does before paying anything.
  2. Standard — one analysis every 24 hours, five projects, and full access to the Forum.
  3. Premium — five analyses every 24 hours, ten projects, the Forum, Pre-Flight, and its own visual theme.
  4. Your remaining analyses are always shown in the right-hand panel, so you never have to count them yourself.

Open Products → Plans in the sidebar to compare them or change plan. A paid plan is paid period by period and never renews on its own: nothing is charged unless you decide to pay again.

Open Products → Plans in the sidebar to compare and upgrade at any time.

Account & Settings

Your profile, your plan, your rank and how the platform looks and speaks to you. All of it changes instantly and none of it affects your saved work.

  1. Open the account panel at the top of the right rail to see your plan, your rank and your codes.
  2. Switch language at any time between English, Spanish, French and Portuguese — the whole platform follows, including the analyses.
  3. Switch between light and dark from the sidebar, and change your theme if you own one: themes restyle the platform without moving anything around.
  4. Watch your rank climb as you earn experience by analyzing, answering quizzes and taking part in the forum. Rank only ever goes up.
  5. Open Products → Settings for your account details, your password and your session.

Reaching a new rank issues a certificate with a public verification page, so it can be shared and checked by anyone. It certifies study on this platform — it is not a professional qualification or a licence.
