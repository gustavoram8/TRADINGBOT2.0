## 1. Who We Are & Scope

Trader Acelerator is a digital educational platform for financial-markets and technical-analysis education. We are the data controller responsible for the personal information processed through the Service. This Policy applies to information we collect through the Service and through communications with us. It does not apply to third-party websites or services we do not control.


## 2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to operate the Service. Specifically:

When you register, we collect your username, email address, and a password (which we store only as a salted cryptographic hash — we never store your password in plain text). We also store your plan tier and its lifecycle dates, your email-verification status and temporary one-time verification codes, the date you accepted our Terms, and account status flags.

For paid plans we create an order record containing the plan, billing cycle, amount, and payment status. Payments are currently processed outside the platform (e.g., bank or other transfer confirmed manually), and we do not collect or store your card numbers or banking credentials on our servers. If we introduce an automated payment processor in the future, your payment-card data would be handled directly by that processor under its own privacy terms, not stored by us.
- Uploaded images (e.g., chart screenshots you submit for AI analysis).
- Forum posts and comments, and reactions.
- Reviews / testimonials you choose to submit (which, with your consent, may be displayed publicly — see Section 7).
- Pre-Flight checklists and self-logged trade details (such as instrument, direction, dates, prices, and notes) that you enter yourself.
- Messages you send us via the contact form (name, email, category, and your message).

To operate engagement features we store your quiz/challenge progress, experience points (XP), rank, badges, streaks, daily-challenge state, and any promotional codes issued to you, together with an append-only log of these reward events for accuracy and anti-abuse purposes.

We do not store your IP address, device fingerprint, or browsing history in our application database, and we do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. As with any website, our hosting and the third-party content-delivery networks that serve fonts and scripts may process your IP address transiently to deliver those resources and maintain security (see Sections 6 and 7).


## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:
- Create and maintain your account and authenticate your logins;
- Provide the educational tools and features of your plan, including AI analysis, quizzes, the Daily Challenge, Pre-Flight, the forum, and gamification;
- Verify your email and send essential service emails (verification codes, password resets, and responses to your support requests);
- Process and activate your subscription and keep billing/plan records;
- Operate and secure reward features and prevent fraud, cheating, and abuse;
- Moderate community content and enforce our Terms;
- Maintain operational and security logs of sensitive events (such as payments and email delivery); and
- Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.


## 4. Legal Bases for Processing

Where applicable law (such as the EU/UK GDPR) requires a legal basis, we rely on: performance of a contract (to provide the Service you sign up for); legitimate interests (to secure the Service, prevent abuse, and improve our features, balanced against your rights); consent (for example, to publish a testimonial you submit, which you may withdraw); and compliance with legal obligations.


## 5. AI Processing of Uploaded Content

When you use AI-assisted features (such as chart-screenshot analysis or community moderation), the image or text you submit is transmitted to a third-party AI processing provider that generates the analysis or moderation result on our behalf. That content is processed to return a result to you and is subject to the provider's own data-handling terms.

Please do not upload sensitive personal information (such as government IDs, financial-account numbers, or other people's personal data) within screenshots or text, as the educational analysis features are not designed to handle such data.


## 6. Cookies & Local Storage

We use a minimal set of strictly necessary cookies and browser storage — we do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies:
- Session cookie: a signed cookie that keeps you logged in during your visit.
- "Remember this device" cookie: an optional signed cookie that keeps you logged in across visits if you choose it.
- Local storage: small preferences kept in your own browser, such as your theme (light/dark), language, accumulated in-app usage time, and locally saved boards. This data stays on your device.

You can clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings; doing so may log you out and reset local preferences.


## 7. How We Share Information — Service Providers

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run the Service, with the categories of providers below, each acting under appropriate confidentiality and data-processing obligations:

Public content: forum posts, comments, and any testimonial you submit with consent to publish are visible to other users or the public, along with the username (or display name) and rank shown with them. Do not post anything you wish to keep private.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to enforce our Terms, to protect the rights, safety, or property of Trader Acelerator or others, or in connection with a business transfer (such as a merger or acquisition).


## 8. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. We keep certain records (such as billing/order records and security or audit logs) for longer where necessary to comply with legal, accounting, or fraud-prevention obligations. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law or for the establishment or defense of legal claims. Public content you posted may remain visible unless you remove it or request its removal.


## 9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including hashing of passwords, cryptographically signed authentication cookies, transport encryption (HTTPS), access controls, and operational logging of sensitive events. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your account credentials confidential and for notifying us of any suspected unauthorized access.


## 10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, update, or delete your personal information; to object to or restrict certain processing; to withdraw consent (for example, for a published testimonial); and to request a portable copy of the information you provided. Where the EU/UK GDPR applies, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

California residents: we do not sell or "share" personal information as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 15. We may need to verify your identity before acting on your request.


## 11. International Data Transfers

Trader Acelerator is operated from, and relies on service providers located in, various countries, which may include the United States, the European Union, and others. If you access the Service from outside those locations, your information may be transferred to and processed in countries whose data-protection laws differ from those of your country. Where required, we take steps to ensure such transfers are subject to appropriate safeguards.


## 12. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended solely for users who are at least 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have collected information from a person under 18, we will delete it. If you believe a minor has provided us information, please contact us.


## 13. Third-Party Links

The Service may contain links to third-party websites or resources. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third parties. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any site you visit.


## 14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you by email or an in-platform notice. Your continued use of the Service after the changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated Policy.
